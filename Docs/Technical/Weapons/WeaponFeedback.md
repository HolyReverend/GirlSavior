# Weapon feedback and HUD

Build on `AGirlSaviorCombatFeedbackPresenter`, `AGirlSaviorCombatHUD`, `WBP_CombatHUD` and the [shot feedback design](../../GameDesign/Mechanics/Weapons/ShotFeedback.md). Use the saved route and its dark-room lighting for visual acceptance.

## Author content

1. In `/Game/Art/Textures/Weapons/`, make three icons with different silhouettes, then assign them to the three rows of `DT_Weapons`. Author three readable collision-free weapon silhouettes under an appropriate `Art` mesh folder. Reuse the pistol shape or mesh geometry from the current presenter where practical. The shotgun uses a broad, long silhouette. The rifle uses a narrower profile with a visible magazine shape.
2. Reuse `SW_PistolShot` under `/Game/Audio/Combat`. Import and assign `SW_ShotgunShot` and `SW_AssaultRifleShot` as distinct short cues. Shotgun audio is one low report. Rifle audio is a short, light impulse that can repeat at `8/s`, or faster when Ida is active, without drowning out threats. Route to the same combat audio path. Set separate shot concurrency from hit and threat cues, with enough voices to preserve each accepted rifle impulse. Tune in game with the current camera and movement.
3. Edit the row `Presentation` fields in `DT_Weapons` and the existing presenter Blueprint. Use the table values at runtime and the existing localized HUD/widget structure. Add `WBP_WeaponLoadout` for Camp and extend the existing `WBP_CombatHUD` for the three compact slot readouts. Validate references after saving and reopening all touched assets.

The numbers below are initial visual tuning, subject to in-engine readability tests. Each flash scale is a factor relative to the migrated pistol muzzle flash. Kick is a local backward visual offset and has no effect on aim.

| Field | Pistol | Shotgun | Assault rifle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sound | Dry isolated report | Low single blast | Light short pulse |
| Flash scale / duration | `1.0 / 0.06 s` | `1.45 / 0.08 s` | `0.65 / 0.04 s` |
| Kick / recovery | `8 cm / 0.12 s` | `14 cm / 0.24 s` | `4 cm / 0.08 s` |
| Trail length / fade | `68 cm / 0.15 s` | `44 cm / 0.12 s` per pellet | `36 cm / 0.08 s` |
| Trail width | `2.0` draw width | `1.5` per pellet | `1.6` draw width |

## Presentation contracts

- On binding a hero, get the active weapon view immediately, then subscribe to `OnWeaponChanged` and `OnWeaponShot`. Reuse the presenter's one held mesh. Apply visual transforms, flash and recoil to the cosmetic mesh or upper-body presentation. Attach to the current aim-aligned mount. Use the existing clipped muzzle/start point between the capsule center and cosmetic muzzle. A block in that segment keeps flash on the hero's side. Keep the camera steady. Reset recoil on switching and unbinding. A switch changes the mesh and HUD even without a subsequent shot.
- On a committed shot, play the row's sound, muzzle flash and one local kick from the shot cue. A shotgun discharge plays once for up to five projectiles. A canceled click and a switch alone emit no shot cue. Move native route listeners from the old position/direction-only combat cue to `OnWeaponShot` and adapt the existing probes. Keep the separate Demo Blueprint fire/impact events working. Give the small combat fixture the standard presenter or verify its equivalent cue path when meaningful for its acceptance.
- Projectiles render trails from their launched presentation snapshot, clamped to traveled distance. On completion, detach a small fade at the actual traveled endpoint. If initial cover stopped the projectile at spawn, leave no trail beyond the blocker. Enemy projectiles retain their visual defaults. Missing presentation state during teardown cannot affect damage or cadence.
- Retain `OnDamagePresentation` as the target cue source and `OnResolvedImpact` as the geometry/ward source. This preserves lethal presentation after structural listeners remove an enemy from the live registry. For a tagged hero shot, limit repeated target cues by `(ShotGroupId, target identity, result kind)`. Track barrier, health and lethal individually. A pellet that touches barrier and health can show both, and a later lethal pellet still shows the kill. Each pellet resolves damage separately. Key geometry feedback separately by weak actor/component and surface, or by world-space contact cell for a synthetic blocker without an actor. Keep contacts on different actors or surfaces distinguishable.
- Copy `WeaponId`, `ShotGroupId` and weak blocker identity into hero projectile impact payloads. Read hero target identity and group from the existing damage presentation callback and `Result.Spec`. On `OnProjectileRegistered`, record the group's expiry from launch game time plus the shared projectile safety lifetime. Registration occurs before the immediate-block callbacks inside spawn. Keep group entries until expiry even when the number of live pellets is temporarily zero, so five pellets stopped at spawn share the cache. Prune expired entries on the presenter tick and clear them at teardown. Contact keys are bounded by the authored pellet count and supported cue kinds.
- Barrier-only results show protection without a body-hit confirmation. Ward, solid geometry, dash protection where supported, and expiry use distinct existing outcomes. For the dark-route fixture, support default and metal/wood/stone contact cues. Assign physical materials to the test surfaces' blocking collision. Set `bReturnMaterialOnMove` on the projectile sphere for its movement sweep, then copy the surface type from `FHitResult::PhysMaterial` into the impact payload. Use the generic geometry effect when a material is unavailable, including synthetic initial-overlap or ward contacts. Keep sounds local and bursts small. Hero aim, threat warnings and the dash path must remain readable with all five trails or a sustained burst. Optional rate limiting of repeated rifle impact sounds must preserve first impact and every lethal cue. ## Expedition HUD

Extend `AGirlSaviorCombatHUD::BindHero` to sample and subscribe to the fire component and unsubscribe from the previous hero. Feed the existing widget native view data for three slot labels, icons/names, and active index. Style the active slot using an outline or marker as well as color. Empty slots remain visible with a dim label. The HUD is `HitTestInvisible`, leaves LMB and aiming to the controller, and follows the existing terminal/Retry/Camp return layers. No ammo count is shown for unlimited ammunition. The weapon strip renders from the actual occupied slots, including a loadout whose first position is empty.
